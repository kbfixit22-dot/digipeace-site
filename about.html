About DigiPeace LLC

Technology that brings a little peace.

DigiPeace LLC is an independent app studio building small, useful and slightly playful apps for everyday life: ElfAnt for tasks and habits, Game Night for keeping score, and Jo Breezy Space Escape for puzzle fun. We also offer career and college readiness reviews that help people get found by the software that now screens them.

Scattered pixels settling into a peace sign: the idea behind everything we deliver.

How we build apps

Free for everyone

Every app is free to download and supported by ads, so no one is left out of game night.

Offline-first, no accounts

Open the app and start. Your tasks, scores and progress live on your phone, and calendar sync is optional, never required.

For all ages

Rated 4+ and designed so kids, parents and grandparents can all play and use them.

A bit of personality

Small touches, like Memo the elephant, make staying on top of things feel lighter instead of like one more chore. We ship updates often and read every review.

How we approach career reviews

Suggestions, not ghostwriting

We show what to change and why. You make the edits, so the voice on your profile stays yours.

Real data, read live

We review your live profile and open every job posting before we list it. No guesses from old templates.

Ranked by impact

Every finding is marked P1, P2 or P3, so you always know which fix matters most.

One standard

Every report shares the same layout, so a family or a team can compare them side by side.
