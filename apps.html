ElfAnt

Never Forget

A friendly, offline-first app for tasks, habits, goals and daily journaling, with Memo, an elephant companion who nudges, celebrates and keeps you accountable. Reminders come 15 minutes before, 5 minutes before and right on time, and you can snooze them.

  • Tasks with priorities, due dates and repeats
  • Habit check-ins and streaks
  • Goals broken into milestones
  • Daily journal with text, photos and GIFs
  • Share a task as a scannable card, no account on either end
  • Pomodoro focus timer
  • Google Calendar sync
  • XP, coins and achievements
  • Weekly review of wins
  • Light and dark mode, backup and restore

Game Night

The scorekeeper for card and table games

Built-in rules for Spades, Hearts, Euchre, Pitch, Scrabble, Cribbage, Dominoes, Rummy 500, Quidler and Yahtzee, plus a custom mode for any game played to a target score. Shareable score cards turn an evening into something worth posting and looking back on.

  • Camera scanning counts your cards
  • Rules applied round by round
  • Head-to-head records and win streaks
  • Photo score cards with stickers and captions
  • Share results friends can import
  • No account; games stay on your device
