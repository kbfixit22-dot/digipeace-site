Common questions

Do you rewrite my resume or profile?

No. We tell you precisely what to change and why, with before/after examples. You make the edits, so your voice stays your own.

How long does a report take?

It depends on the service. We confirm your delivery date when you book.

What do I need to send?

Your LinkedIn URL, your current resume as a .docx, and the roles you're targeting. For college plans: the student's list, intended majors and deadlines.

Can I bundle services?

Yes. Ask about combining services, such as a LinkedIn and Resume review together, when you get in touch.
